# Verbatim clause checks (read-directly, built-in browser, 2026-11-18)

Access: read-directly (full page innerText, EDGAR). Quotes trimmed but not reworded.

## Wolverine World Wide — 2025 replacement facility (CONFIRMED: R route open)
- Blocker, last para of 7.5: "no Material Intellectual Property shall be permitted to be sold, disposed of, invested or otherwise transferred (including by way of license or sublicense) to any Unrestricted Subsidiary" — U only.
- 7.17: IP Reorganization (to wholly-owned Foreign Subsidiaries) allowed if pro forma Consolidated Leverage < 2.50x (after a 1-year / term-loan-repayment lockout), OR if the IP moved to non-guarantors is <= 67.0% of total IP value "as reasonably determined by Parent Borrower".
- Material IP is defined objectively ("material to the operation of the business"), with no named decider.

## JOANN — 2024 exit credit agreement (CONFIRMED: R and U closed; JV route needs an FMV sale)
- Material IP: "owned by any Loan Party ... (as reasonably determined by the Borrower in good faith)". IP already outside the Loan Parties is not covered.
- 7.02/7.05/7.06: no Investment, Disposition or RP of Material IP "to any Subsidiary (other than another Loan Party)". Exclusive distribution licenses are caught.
- 6.14: no transfer to an Unrestricted Subsidiary; no designation of an IP owner.
- Non-subsidiary JVs are not named. A disposition to a JV must be at FMV under 7.05 proviso (ii).

## GEO Group — 2022 A&R credit agreement (CONFIRMED: screen sits on one basket)
- "Specified Investment Conditions" (applies to 6.04(j) only): the Investment "may not consist of Material Intellectual Property or Equity Interests in any Subsidiary that owns any Material Intellectual Property".
- 6.03(g): GEO may "sell or otherwise dispose of assets (including to Affiliates, subject to Section 6.06)" for cash at fair market value, with no IP screen.
- Unusual feature: Material IP is "as reasonably determined by the Required Lenders". Changing it requires each Lender's consent (9.02(b)(ii)(N)).

## Weber — 2021 credit agreement (CONFIRMED closed; soft spot = "ordinary course")
- Last para of 6.05: "in no event shall any Loan Party be permitted to dispose of any Material Intellectual Property, whether as a Disposition, Investment, Restricted Payment or otherwise, except in the ordinary course of such Loan Party's business."
- 6.04(b): no intercompany Investment "shall result in any Subsidiary that is not a Loan Party owning Material Intellectual Property". 5.10(c) and the Unrestricted Subsidiary definition bar U from owning Material IP.
- 5.13(ii) carve-out ("Disposition ... otherwise permitted") appears overridden by the 6.05 notwithstanding (reasoning, not read in a court ruling).

## Designer Brands — 2023 term credit agreement (CONFIRMED closed, consent gate)
- 6.02 and 6.04 tails: no Investment, RP or Disposition (other than to a Loan Party) of Material IP, Material Real Property or IPCo JV equity "owned by the Company or another Loan Party ... without the prior written consent of the Administrative Agent". Also covers non-IP assets (real property, JV equity).
- Material IP is owned by a Loan Party only, so it does not follow the IP once it has left with consent.

## New Fortress revolver — 2025 conformed (CONFIRMED fully closed; earlier ambiguity resolved)
- Material IP: owned by "the Borrower or any Restricted Subsidiary", so the protection follows the IP.
- Designation paragraph: no "sale ... (including by way of an exclusive license), contribution, dividend or other investment of any Material Intellectual Property or FLNG1 Collateral or any FLNG2 Collateral to any entity other than a Loan Party". This closes R, U and N for the IP itself, independently of 6.1(e).
- Also protects non-IP crown jewels (the FLNG vessels).

## Blue Bird — 2021 Fourth Amendment (CONFIRMED closed)
- New 6.14: no disposition, investment, dividend, licence etc. of Material IP other than (x) to a Loan Party, (y) between non-Guarantor Restricted Subsidiaries, or (z) non-exclusive ordinary-course licences. Transfers include selling an IP-owner's equity and making an IP owner an Excluded Subsidiary.
- Material IP covers IP of "any Loan Party or any Restricted Subsidiary". An Unrestricted Subsidiary that comes to own Material IP automatically becomes Restricted.

## Mallinckrodt — 2022 Opioid Deferred Cash Payments Agreement (CONFIRMED: R, U closed; JV not named)
- Material IP: "owned by any Settlement Party", so the protection does not follow the IP after it leaves.
- 6.04, 6.05 and 6.06 tails: no Investment, Disposition or RP of Material IP to "any Subsidiary (other than another Settlement Party) or any Unrestricted Subsidiary". Exclusive distribution licences are caught.
- Non-subsidiary JVs are not named (same template as JOANN).

## V2X — Jan 2025 repricing / conformed CA (CONFIRMED: U only; R open)
- "neither the Borrower nor any Restricted Subsidiary shall be permitted to contribute, sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of any Material Intellectual Property or Contracts to an Unrestricted Subsidiary"; designation of a holder is also barred.
- Covers customer CONTRACTS as well as IP, with materiality by "good faith determination of the Borrower". Non-guarantor restricted subsidiaries are not covered.

## Chefs' Warehouse — amended TL credit agreement, 2022 (CONFIRMED: U only; R open)
- "(iv) no Unrestricted Subsidiary may own (or be licensee of) any material intellectual property or other material assets ... at any time". It covers all material assets, but only for unrestricted subsidiaries.

- Block 6.08(b): "Investments by the Borrower in or to any Restricted Subsidiary and by any Restricted Subsidiary in or to the Borrower or any other Restricted Subsidiary" — no cap. 6.08(c) general basket: greater of $3,300,000,000 and 100% of Consolidated EBITDA (wave 1 used $991M from an earlier version; capacity is understated, not overstated). CONFIRMED UNBOUNDED.
- Pagaya, "Permitted Investment" (a): Investments by the Borrower or its Subsidiaries in "a Subsidiary ... (other than an SPV Subsidiary)" — no cap. CONFIRMED UNBOUNDED.
- Clear Channel 7.02(d)(iv): Loan Party -> non-Loan Party Restricted Subsidiary either "(x) ... in the ordinary course or consistent with past practice or (y) not exceed the greater of (x) $150,000,00[0 ...]". Unbounded only through the ordinary-course / past-practice prong. QUALIFIED: soft-uncapped.
- Farfetch, Permitted Investments (ii): "any Investment in the Parent or any Restricted Subsidiary; provided that any Investment by a Loan Party in a Non-Loan Party, to the extent outstanding for more than 90 days, shall be secured in favor of the Secured Parties". Uncapped, but must be SECURED after 90 days. QUALIFIED: the dollars can move, but lenders keep a claim. Reclassify from "unbounded leak" to "uncapped but secured".
